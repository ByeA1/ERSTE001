07 — PHYSICAL PROTOTYPING

The transition from digital CAD to a physical object is rarely seamless. To validate structural integrity and assembly tolerances, a functional prototype was manufactured using MJF (Multi Jet Fusion) PA12. This iteration was not intended to represent final injection-molded production, but served as a critical engineering checkpoint.

The physical evaluation followed a rigorous loop: Prototype → Test → Discover → Refine. By examining the raw printed nylon, critical insights were gathered regarding thin-wall limitations, surface defects, and the tactile rigidity of the Sensor Spine assembly.
