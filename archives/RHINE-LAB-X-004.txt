三维结构档案用于阅读零件之间的空间关系。开放式底部与完整爆炸展示装配层次，侧向实体剖切揭示上盖内部类工字形加强筋，按键盖独立翻转展示内侧筋位。电子件仅用于表达装配占位与相对位置。

研究记录
1. 无传统底壳的开放式底部保留内部结构的可见性；爆炸状态用于辨认分件和装配关系。
2. 实体剖切观察截面厚度、筋板与侧壁连接；按键盖内侧特写观察交叉筋和薄壁连接。
3. 主板、电池等电子件为参考模型，不能据此确认最终硬件规格、装配公差或整机重量。

ABSTRACT
This 3D record explains spatial relationships between parts. The open underside and full exploded assembly show assembly layers. A lateral solid-section cut reveals I-like reinforcement ribs inside the upper shell, while an independently flipped button cover exposes its internal ribs. Electronic components serve only as references for occupied space and relative placement.

RESEARCH NOTES
1. The open underside has no conventional bottom shell and leaves internal structures visible. The exploded state clarifies part divisions and assembly relationships.
2. Solid-section views show section thickness and rib-to-sidewall connections. Interior button-cover close-ups show cross ribs and their thin-wall connections.
3. The mainboard, battery and other electronics are reference models. They do not establish final hardware specifications, assembly tolerances or total product weight.
