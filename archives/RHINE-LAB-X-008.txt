ABSTRACT
Early ERSTE002 records studied space rearrangement within a low-profile shell and a closed base plate. The current portfolio presents a version with no conventional bottom shell or separate central spine, focusing on right-hand asymmetric surfaces and integral structural reinforcement. Early-version records do not describe the current structure.

RESEARCH NOTES
1. The closed base plate and space rearrangement belong to earlier iterations and must not be used to describe the current model.
2. The current presentation baseline is a structure without a conventional bottom shell, using integral reinforcement ribs and transverse supports.
3. Approximately 33 g remains a design target. Physical weight, structural performance and final hardware fit require subsequent physical validation of the corresponding version.
